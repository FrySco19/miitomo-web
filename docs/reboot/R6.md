# Reboot R6 — progress

The plan and its reasons are in [R6-AUDIT.md](R6-AUDIT.md). This page records what is actually done, block by block.

Test levels used below:

- **unit tested**: model tests in `tests/unit`;
- **UI smoke tested**: the real Reboot runtime in Chromium on the engine test double (`tests/e2e/r5-ui.smoke.mjs`);
- **real-engine tested**: with the user's Miitomo files (`tests/e2e/r5-social.smoke.mjs` and the Classic / R2–R4
  suites). These run on the maintainer's computer, not in the cloud;
- **not verified**.

## R6.0a — block 1: design system + Settings

### Implemented

**Design system** — see [DESIGN-SYSTEM.md](DESIGN-SYSTEM.md).

- Tokens and shared components (`reboot/ui/tokens.css`).
- Every R1–R5 Reboot stylesheet now uses them: Reboot panel, Piazza, friends' rooms, place bar, Sala Giochi,
  MiiPhone, MIInstagram.
- Contrast was checked; reduced motion is respected.

**Languages**

- Locales come from a registry. A language appears in Settings as soon as `strings.ts` has a table for it.
- Native names are ready for es / fr / de / pt / ja. These languages are **not offered yet**: they have no table, and
  translating them is not part of this block.
- A **region** (automatic, or chosen) decides how dates are written. Example: the birthday question of the Mii Quiz
  writes 3 January as 03/01 in Italy (day first) and 1/3 in the US (month first).

**Settings** (MiiPhone → Settings). Every option has a real effect:

| Option | Effect |
|---|---|
| Your Mii's name | Saved to the profile and the Mii nickname (max 10; an empty name is refused) |
| Language | The whole Reboot UI switches at once |
| Region | Date format |
| Music / Effects / Mii voices | Volume 0–100 %, on top of the original on/off switches |
| 3D quality | Auto / High / Balanced / Low. This is the pixel-ratio cap of the 3D view: 2 / 1.5 / 1 |
| Animations | As the device / All / Reduced |
| Light mode | Low quality and reduced motion. The simulated world is calmer: arrivals come twice as far apart, and the Miis post half as often |
| Ask before acting | Leaving a place needs a second tap; deleting a post asks first |
| Notifications | The number on the MiiPhone (dock and app tile), and who comes and goes in places |

- Settings are validated whenever they are loaded or changed, and stored in `mtw.reboot.settings`.
- If storage is full or missing, the settings still apply for this session.
- When Reboot is disposed, it gives back the Classic values: volume 1, quality high.

**MiiPhone**

- New home screen: clock, date, coloured app tiles, number badges.
- The app model can now hold shortcuts: an app that performs a real move and closes the phone. A refused move keeps
  the phone open and shows the reason. These shortcuts are used by the next block.

**MIInstagram**

- You can delete your own posts. It asks first when confirmations are on.

**Shared engine hooks** (only Reboot calls them; Classic keeps its behaviour):

- `RoomSound.setLevels`;
- the browser voice level;
- `setRenderQuality`;
- `setPlayerName`.

### Tested

**Unit tested**

- `tests/unit/settings.test.mts`, 30 checks:
  - validation and clamping;
  - persistence, corrupt or full storage;
  - copy-on-read and reset;
  - effective values for each option;
  - the locale registry, region and date format;
  - MiiPhone shortcuts (success and refusal) and badges.
- `tests/unit/i18n.test.mts` now also checks:
  - the `set.*` key families;
  - no hard-coded text in the Settings view.
- All earlier suites still pass.

**UI smoke tested** (`r5-ui.smoke.mjs`, 43 checks at the end of this block). The R6 checks:

- Settings app:
  - music 40 % reaches the host;
  - quality Low reaches the host;
  - region GB gives the `en-GB` date format;
  - confirmations off;
  - everything is stored.
- Player name: an empty name is refused; a new name is saved.
- Language switched from Settings; the phone changes language at once.
- Light mode on, then off: everything goes back exactly as it was.
- Settings survive a reload and are applied at start.
- Leaving a place: the first tap arms the button, the second tap leaves.
- Deleting a post: with confirmations on, "keep it" keeps the post; with confirmations off, the post goes at once.
- Classic `?reboot=0` still loads no Reboot code.

**Real-engine tested**: not yet. To be run on the PC:

- volumes on the real BGM and effects;
- the browser voice level;
- the pixel ratio on the real renderer;
- the name change in the real save.

### Known limitations

- Only English and Italian have tables.
- The MiiPhone home redraws its badges when the phone opens, not while it is already open.
- (Solved in block 2: the Reboot panel and the chat lines are translated; the voice follows each line's language.)

## R6.0a — block 2: My page, Friends, shortcuts, translated panel and chat

### Implemented

**MiiPhone as the hub of Reboot.** The home screen shows, in this order: My page, Friends, MIInstagram, Plaza, Home,
Rooms, Games, Shop (R6.0b), Settings. Only apps that work are shown (no Messages tile until messages exist).

- **My page**, three tabs:
  - *Me*: the user's Mii (name, personality, birthday as day and month — the save has no year —, greeting), real
    counters (posts, friends, matches, wins, coins) and the badges won;
  - *Posts*: the user's own MIInstagram posts (tap → MIInstagram);
  - *Friends*: the five closest friends with their level (→ the Friends app).
  A missing fact shows "not set"; a system that is switched off hides its part.
- **Friends**: every local friend, closest first, with:
  - a **relationship level** (Friend → Good friend → Close friend → Best friend, from 0.35 / 0.5 / 0.7 / 0.9 of the
    R5 Relations value, which only real things move: chats, visits, matches, comments, reactions) and the progress
    to the next level;
  - **where they are now**, read from the places (their open room, another friend's room, the Sala Giochi, out, at
    home with a private room);
  - **Visit / Join**: a real move through the Piazza; a refused place (full, closed, private) says why.
- **Shortcuts** (a tap moves for real, through the Piazza model, then the phone closes): Plaza, Home, Rooms (the
  Plaza with the friends' rooms list open), Games. A refused move keeps the phone open and shows the Piazza's reason.

**Translations**

- The Reboot panel (several Miis, public room, visitors, events, feature names) is in English and Italian.
- The public room's template title follows the language ("Open lounge" / "Salotto aperto"); a custom title stays.
- The Miis' **chat lines** are in the i18n table: the Miis talk in the Reboot language. Every language has the same
  keys, so the same seed picks the same line.
- **Voice**: each Reboot chat balloon tells the voice its language, so English lines get an English voice. Classic
  balloons (original dialogue) keep the Italian voice.

### Tested

- **Unit**:
  - `people.test.mts` (16 checks): levels and thresholds, real bumps raising a level, `whereIs` consistent with
    presence for every friend, a visit through the real SocialHub (success, a full room refused with a reason, back
    home with no presence left), own posts for My page, translated template titles;
  - `social.test.mts`: lines in English with `lang: 'en'`, Italian with no language (as R2b), same first pick;
  - `i18n.test.mts`: all the new key families (panel events, feature names, levels, places, chat pools).
- **UI smoke** (`r5-ui.smoke.mjs`):
  - the home screen lists exactly the real apps;
  - My page shows the real name and counters, one tile per own post, the 5 closest friends;
  - Friends lists 7 friends whose "where" matches the places; Visit puts the user inside that place, phone closed;
  - Plaza leaves the place with cleanup; Rooms opens the friends' rooms list; Games enters the Sala Giochi; Home
    returns home with nothing left behind;
  - the Reboot panel is in English after the language switch.
- **Real engine**: not yet. To check on the PC: an English chat line with an English voice in the real room; the
  shortcuts with the real scene changes.

## R6.0b — Reboot economy + shop

See [ECONOMY.md](ECONOMY.md).

### Implemented

- **Coins** (NEW design; no real money; Classic's shop stays a catalogue because Miitomo's prices were server data).
  Earned only by real events, through named rules with daily caps and once-only keys: welcome, first visit of the
  day, a match, a win, a new badge, a photo posted, a friend's room visited, a friend getting closer. Every change is
  in a history. A coin notice appears when notifications are on.
- **Reboot shop** (MiiPhone → Shop): new looks drawn in CSS for Reboot, each with a visible effect:
  - phone covers (the MiiPhone on the screen edge and the open phone) — a cover can be tried on the phone before
    buying; the try-on ends when the shop closes;
  - profile cards (the background of the card on My page);
  - photo frames (around the user's own photos on MIInstagram).
  Buy (a second tap when confirmations are on), use, already owned, not enough coins ("N more needed").
  Wallet tab: balance, how to earn with today's use, history.
- **Clothes are not sold**: the Classic closet already lets the user wear every item of their wardrobe files;
  selling them would only lock what is free. Furniture joins the shop with the house (R6.1).
- Feature flag `SHOP_ENABLED` (Reboot panel).

### Tested

- **Unit** — `economy.test.mts` (26 checks): rules, daily caps, once keys, a new day, spend refusals (insufficient,
  invalid), history, persistence, corrupt / negative / full storage; catalogue checks (ids, one free starter per
  kind, prices, names in every language), starters, buy / equip / refusals, a stored record checked on load.
- **UI smoke**: coins come from the real events of the run (welcome, daily, photo, visit); the balance shown matches;
  a cover tried on the phone; buying asks first, then spends 40 and recolours the phone; the history names the
  purchase; closing the shop ends the try-on; a finished match pays once; coins and cover survive a reload.
  The whole UI smoke run now has 61 checks, all passing.
- **Real engine**: the shop involves no engine code. The rewards of a match or a visit in the real 3D room are not
  verified yet (same events as in the UI smoke).

### Known limitations

- Local only: the wallet is in this browser's storage. Someone who edits storage can give themselves coins; nothing
  depends on it (no real money, no advantage). Our server (R10) will validate every grant and spend.
- The shop has 14 items (6 covers, 4 cards, 4 frames, including the 3 free starters).
